Privacy Policy

Clash Lab Fitness LLC · CLF Protocol · Last updated November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the CLF Protocol app or submit an onboarding application at clashlabfitness.com, we collect only the information needed to deliver 1-on-1 remote personal training:

  • Account & Profile Info: Name, email address, age, height, starting weight, goal weight, equipment availability, and training schedule.
  • Health & Fitness Data: Workout sets, reps, weights, daily check-ins (weight, calories, protein, water, steps, sleep, energy, joint comfort), body measurements, PAR-Q+ health screening responses, and optional progress photos you choose to upload.
  • Optional Wearable Sync (Apple Health / Health Connect): Only if you explicitly enable syncing, we read bodyweight, step count, sleep duration, and active calories to populate your daily check-in log. We never sell or share wearable health data with third parties or advertisers.
  • Payments: All payments are processed securely by Stripe. Clash Lab Fitness LLC never stores your full credit card number.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely by your coach at Clash Lab Fitness LLC to engineer your training splits, track your strength trajectory, adjust nutrition targets, and communicate with you inside the app.

3. Data Storage & Security

All account, workout, check-in, and private progress photo data is stored securely in Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) so only you and your assigned coach can access your records. All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You may delete your account and associated personal data at any time directly inside the CLF Protocol app under Settings → Delete my account, or by emailing support@clashlabfitness.com. Deletion requests are processed immediately (or within 48 hours via email).

5. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@clashlabfitness.com.
