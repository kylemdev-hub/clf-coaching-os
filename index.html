FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a commercial gym, or can I train at home?
Both work great. During your intake, you list the exact equipment you have access to — whether it's a commercial gym, a garage barbell setup, or dumbbells and bands — and Kyle builds your split around your exact gear.
What's the difference between the $100 8-Week Intensive and the $50/mo Membership?
The $100 8-Week Intensive is a one-time payment for a complete 8-week custom split, nutrition protocol, and full CLF Protocol app access — ideal if you want a structured block with zero recurring charges. The $50/mo Ongoing Membership includes everything in the 8-Week Intensive plus automatic Phase Gate reviews, fresh splits every 8 weeks, ongoing macro adjustments, and cancel-anytime flexibility.
How do I access the CLF Protocol app on my phone?
Once your program is built, you sign in at CLF Protocol using your email magic link or password (or via our mobile app). Everything — your workouts, rest timer, PR badges, daily check-ins, progress photos, and 1-on-1 coach chat — syncs automatically.
What happens if I answer "Yes" on the PAR-Q+ health screening?
Your safety comes first. If you flag an item on the PAR-Q+, we won't charge you right away — we'll ask for a quick physician clearance note sent to support@clashlabfitness.com before unlocking your enrollment and training split.
How fast will I get my custom program after applying?
Once your application and 3-day food log are submitted, Kyle personally reviews your intake and builds your custom split and macro targets within 3–5 business days.
Can I cancel my $50/month membership anytime?
Yes — zero contracts and zero lock-in. Just message Kyle in the app or email support@clashlabfitness.com anytime before your next billing date.
