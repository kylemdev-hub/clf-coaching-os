FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a commercial gym, or can I train at home?
Both work great. During your intake, you list the exact equipment you have on hand (a commercial gym, a garage barbell setup, or dumbbells and bands), and Kyle builds your split around your exact setup and available time.
What's the difference between the $100 8-Week Intensive and the $50/mo Membership?
The $100 8-Week Intensive is a one-time payment for a complete 8-week custom split, nutrition targets, and full CLF Protocol app access, which is great if you want one focused block with no recurring charge. The $50/mo Ongoing Membership includes everything in the 8-Week Intensive plus fresh splits every 8 weeks, ongoing macro adjustments, and cancel-anytime flexibility.
How do I access the CLF Protocol app on my phone?
Once your program is built, you sign in at CLF Protocol using your email magic link or password (or via our Android app). Your workouts, rest timer, PR badges, daily check-ins, supplement reminders, progress photos, and 1-on-1 coach chat all sync automatically.
What happens if I answer "Yes" on the PAR-Q+ health screening?
Your safety comes first. If you flag an item on the PAR-Q+, you won't be charged right away. Instead, we'll ask for a brief physician clearance note sent to support@clashlabfitness.com before unlocking your enrollment and Week 1 split.
How fast will I get my custom program after applying?
Once your application and 3-day food log are submitted, Kyle personally reviews your intake and builds your custom split and macro targets within 3 to 5 business days.
Can I cancel my $50/month membership anytime?
Yes, there are zero contracts and zero lock-in. Just message Kyle in the app or email support@clashlabfitness.com anytime before your next billing date.
