Need help signing in, syncing Apple Health, updating your training split, or managing your account? We typically respond within 24 hours.

How do I sign in?

Open the CLF Protocol app, tap Email Magic Link, and enter the email address you used during onboarding. Tap the link in your inbox to load your personalized program.

How do I delete my account or data?

Open the app, tap the status pill in the top-right corner to open Settings, and click Delete my account — or email support@clashlabfitness.com.

Medical & Coaching Questions
