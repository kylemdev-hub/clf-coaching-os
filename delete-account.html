Account & Data Deletion — CLF Protocol

You can permanently delete your CLF Protocol (Clash Lab Fitness LLC) account and associated data at any time using either method below.

Option 1: Delete directly in the app (Instant)

  1. Open the CLF Protocol app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (No login required)

If you cannot access the app or only want specific data removed (such as progress photos, check-in logs, or workout history), email support@clashlabfitness.com from your account email address. Requests are completed within 7 business days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account profile, email address, and login credentials
  • Assigned training programs, workout logs, and strength history
  • Daily check-ins, body-weight entries, progress photos, and coach messages

What data may be retained

Payment and billing records processed by Stripe are retained only as required for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention compliance.
