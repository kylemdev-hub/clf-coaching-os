About Clash Lab Fitness

Built from the ground up — zero fluff, real progression.

I'm Kyle, founder of Clash Lab Fitness LLC and an ISSA Certified Personal Trainer.

I didn't start out naturally big or strong. Growing up, I was the skinny kid who just wanted to put on real muscle and feel capable in my own skin. In 2011, I joined the U.S. Air Force, picked up a barbell for the first time with real intent, and never looked back. Over the past decade-plus of training, I learned firsthand what actually drives results — and how much time people waste on random workouts, cookie-cutter PDFs, and unsustainable diets.

Outside the weight room, I spend my spare time training Mixed Martial Arts — with Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and grappling at the top of my list. Combat sports completely shape how I coach: building muscle and moving heavy weight means nothing if your joints feel wrecked or your conditioning falls apart. Every protocol I write balances strength, hypertrophy, and joint longevity so you look athletic and actually perform like it.

I built CLF Protocol so remote coaching feels like having a coach standing next to you — your exact split, form cues, PR tracking, daily check-ins, and direct 1-on-1 messaging all in one distraction-free app.

Coaching Philosophy

How we engineer your protocol.

Every client starts with an intake, a PAR-Q+ health screen, and a 3-day food log — never a recycled template.

01 / INDIVIDUALIZED PROGRAMMING

Built around your body, gear & schedule

Whether you train 3 days a week in a garage gym or 5 days in a full commercial facility, every movement is selected around your equipment, weak points, and past injuries.

02 / MEASURED PROGRESSION

Data-backed overload every session

Inside your CLF Protocol app, every working set is logged with automatic PR detection, next-session target recommendations, and an 8-week strength trajectory curve.

03 / SUSTAINABLE NUTRITION

Macros that fit real life

We start from your real 3-day food log — not an unrealistic crash diet — and dial in protein, calories, and hydration so you can build muscle or lean out without burning out.

04 / 8-WEEK PHASE GATES

Structured blocks & direct coach access

Training runs in focused 8-week blocks with direct 1-on-1 chat, pinned weekly coaching notes, form video feedback, and a full Phase Gate review before your next block begins.
