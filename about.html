About Coach Kyle

Built for real life, real schedules, and real progression.

I'm Kyle de Vroede, founder of Clash Lab Fitness LLC and an ISSA Certified Personal Trainer. Before anything else, I'm a husband and a father to three wonderful boys.

Raising three boys makes one thing obvious fast: time isn't an abundant resource. Most of us don't have two hours a day to live in a gym or weigh out ten complicated meals. That's why I program every workout, nutrition target, and resource here for time and efficiency, so you can get in, do the work that actually moves the needle, and get back to the people who matter most.

I didn't start out naturally big or strong. Growing up, I was the skinny kid trying to figure out how to put on real muscle and feel capable in my own skin. In 2011, I joined the U.S. Air Force, picked up a barbell with real intent, and stayed hooked. Over the past decade-plus of lifting, I learned firsthand what actually builds strength and muscle, and how much time gets wasted on random workouts and crash diets.

Outside of family life and the weight room, I train Mixed Martial Arts, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and grappling. Time on the mats shapes how I coach: moving heavy weight doesn't mean much if your joints ache or your conditioning falls apart. Every split I write balances strength and muscle growth with joint longevity and real conditioning.

I built the CLF Protocol app to take the guesswork out of busy weeks. You open your phone, see your exact lifts, rep targets, form demos, macros, and daily supplement reminders, log your sets in seconds, and message me 1-on-1 whenever your schedule shifts.

How I Coach

Smart programming that respects your clock.

Every client starts with a personal intake, a PAR-Q+ health screen, and a 3-day food log so your plan fits your actual week from Day 1.

01 / TIME-EFFICIENT TRAINING

Maximum return on every gym hour

Whether you have 45 minutes three days a week in a garage gym or five days at a commercial gym, every movement is chosen around your schedule, equipment, and joint history so no set is wasted.

02 / CLEAR WEEKLY TARGETS

Know what to lift before you walk in

Inside the CLF Protocol app, every working set logs in seconds with automatic PR tracking, next-session weight targets, and an 8-week strength trendline.

03 / PRACTICAL NUTRITION & SUPPLEMENTS

Macros that work at the family dinner table

We start from your normal 3-day food log rather than a restrictive crash diet, dialing in realistic protein, calories, hydration, and daily supplement reminders you can keep up with long term.

04 / 8-WEEK COACHING BLOCKS

Direct 1-on-1 access when life gets busy

Training runs in focused 8-week blocks with direct 1-on-1 chat, weekly check-ins, form video reviews, and a full check-up before your next block starts.
